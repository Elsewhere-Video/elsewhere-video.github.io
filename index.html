Elsewhere Video

Quiet places to leave on in the background.

Elsewhere Video makes long, calm ambient videos: candlelit rooms, quiet lobbies, crackling fires and soft rain, usually three hours long with no music, no talking and no sudden sounds. They're made to run on a TV while you read, work, sleep or host a get-together.

The visuals are AI-generated and marked as such on YouTube. Sound beds are freely licensed recordings, mostly CC0.
