Privacy Policy

Elsewhere Video Automation · Last updated 31 October 2026

Elsewhere Video Automation is a private tool used only by the owner of the Elsewhere Video YouTube channel to upload and archive that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's own channel, sets their titles, descriptions and visibility, and reads back their status.
  • Google Drive: stores backup copies of those videos in a folder the tool created, and reads only files it created itself.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows data protection. They are not sent anywhere except to Google to perform the actions above.

What it doesn't do

It collects no data about viewers or anyone else, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and shares or sells nothing to third parties. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Elsewhere Video YouTube channel.
